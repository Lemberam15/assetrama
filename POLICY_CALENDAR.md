# POLICY CALENDAR — RBI MPC & US FOMC (Asset Rama)

**Purpose:** these are the highest-stakes posts we make. The monitors and the
Policy Outcome Watch task read this file so a rate decision is never missed and
never posted late. Ram's rule (07/10/2026): keep the dates and times in advance,
run around the announcement time to capture the accurate outcome, post promptly
(delay loses follower confidence), and double-check before posting.

## How to use this file

1. Before any monitor check, look at the dates below. If TODAY is a decision
   date, that changes your priority: a policy outcome outranks every other
   candidate story that day.
2. Post within ~1 hour of the official announcement — not hours later.
3. DOUBLE-CHECK before posting (mandatory, no exceptions):
   - the rate figure and the change in basis points,
   - the stance wording (e.g. neutral / calibrated tightening / easing),
   - the vote split if stated,
   - the inflation and GDP projections if given.
   Confirm from TWO independent authoritative sources — the central bank's own
   release (rbi.org.in / federalreserve.gov) plus a wire or major outlet
   (PTI, Reuters, CNBC-TV18, Moneycontrol, Mint, Business Standard, The Hindu).
   If the two disagree, DO NOT POST — wait for the official release and check again.
   NEVER post an expected/leaked number as if it were the outcome.
4. Always state the announcement date and time in the post so readers can verify.
