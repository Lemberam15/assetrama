HRA Calculator

How much of your house rent allowance is actually tax-free? Enter your basic salary, HRA and rent, pick your city, and see the exemption the least-of-three rule produces for tax year 2026-27.

How to read it

The smallest of three numbers wins.

Your exemption is not a percentage of your HRA — it is the least of three separate limits. Slide the rent up and watch which limb binds: below 10% of your salary the rent limb is zero, so nothing is exempt at all.

  • Actual HRA — what your payslip shows for the year.
  • Rent minus 10% of salary — the limb that usually binds.
  • 50% / 40% of salary — 50% in eight listed cities, 40% everywhere else.

The rule behind the numbers

Exempt HRALeast of: HRA received · (rent − 10% of salary) · 50% or 40% of salary
Salary = basic + DA (if counted for retirement) + turnover commission. Never CTC.

This is rule 279 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026, in force from 1 April 2026 — what used to be section 10(13A). The formula is unchanged; the city table is not. Eight cities now carry the 50% limit: Mumbai, Kolkata, Delhi, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad and Bengaluru. Everywhere else stays at 40%, including Gurugram, Noida and Navi Mumbai.

Example: basic ₹60,000 a month, HRA ₹24,000, rent ₹25,000, in Bengaluru. Actual HRA is ₹2,88,000, rent minus 10% of salary is ₹2,28,000, and 50% of salary is ₹3,60,000 — so ₹2,28,000 is exempt and ₹60,000 of the allowance is taxed as salary. Drop the rent to ₹5,000 and the exemption becomes nil, because the rent limb falls below zero.

The exemption only exists in the old regime, so the question that decides whether any of this matters is which regime is cheaper for you. Frequently asked questions

How is HRA exemption calculated?

It is the least of three amounts: the HRA you actually received, the rent you paid minus 10% of your salary, and 50% of your salary if the home is in one of the eight listed cities or 40% elsewhere. The smallest of the three is exempt; the rest of the allowance is taxed as salary.

Which cities get the 50% limit?

From tax year 2026-27, rule 279 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026 lists eight: Mumbai, Kolkata, Delhi, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad and Bengaluru. Every other place gets 40%. A city not named in the rule is a 40% place however large it is.

Is HRA exemption available in the new tax regime?

No. The new regime, the default since FY 2023-24, does not allow the HRA exemption at all. You must opt for the old regime to claim it, and for most salaried people the new regime's lower slabs still win — compute both.

What counts as salary here?

Basic pay plus dearness allowance where your terms of employment count it for retirement benefits, plus commission at a fixed percentage of turnover. Bonus, the HRA itself and every other allowance and perquisite are excluded. Using CTC inflates all three limbs.

What if I moved city or changed rent mid-year?

Run the test separately for each period in which the facts stayed the same — a move from a 40% city to a 50% city means two computations, each with its own salary and rent for those months.
