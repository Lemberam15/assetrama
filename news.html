Indian markets staged a sharp rebound on Wednesday, 25 November, clawing back all of Monday’s fall in the first two hours. By about 11:35 am the Sensex was up 2,020.66 points, or 2.45%, at 84,456.65 and the Nifty 50 up 588.70 points, or 2.32%, at 25,938.35. Private banks, banks, IT and FMCG led; media and metal lagged. Three things drove it: steady foreign inflows, a firmer rupee at 92.62 a dollar and Brent slipping back below $91 a barrel. Breadth stayed weak, though — more shares fell than rose on the BSE. Overnight in the US the AI rally cooled again: the Nasdaq fell 1.48% to 23,733.54 and the S&P 500 lost 0.50% to 7,201.09, while the Dow added 0.85% to 50,018.70. Bullion stayed soft, with MCX December gold at ₹1,22,035 per 10 grams and silver at ₹1,45,150 a kilogram in morning trade.
